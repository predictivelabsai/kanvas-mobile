# App access instructions

## Recommended Play Console answer

**All or some functionality is restricted:** Yes.

Core chat and the public information screens can be opened without signing in. An account is required
to review saved sessions, profile settings, notification preferences, and the in-app account-deletion
flow.

## Reviewer instructions

1. Launch the app. The initial screen is **Kanvas AI**.
2. Core AI chat can be tested without an account: choose a suggested prompt or enter an art-market
   question.
3. Open the menu and choose **Sign in** to test account-only functionality.
4. Enter the dedicated review account email and password stored only in Play Console's sign-in-details
   fields.
5. No OTP, subscription, payment, location restriction, or additional approval is required for the
   review account.
6. Open **Profile & Preferences** to review account data controls and the deletion path.
7. Use **Report response** below an AI answer to test the in-app content-reporting mechanism.

Create a dedicated review account immediately before completing Play Console. Do not commit its
password here. Confirm that it can sign in from a clean install and that it contains no personal data.
